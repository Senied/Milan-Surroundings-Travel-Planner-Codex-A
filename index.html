Journey

One base, many directions.

Historic Milan

Start with the Duomo and rooftop, cross the Galleria, continue through Brera and finish at Sforza Castle or the Last Supper.

Neighbourhoods and design · 5–7 hours

Navigli and Tortona

Move from design studios and independent spaces into a slower canal-side evening, with room for aperitivo and an unhurried dinner.

Begin in the afternoon
Pace

Lake and ferry · 10–12 hours

Varenna and Bellagio

Take the direct train to Varenna, cross the lake by ferry and let the boat timetable shape a balanced day between stone lanes and waterfront gardens.

Best for
